# Executive Decision Report & Capstone Presentation

## Business Intelligence & Data Analytics

### Project Overview
This project transforms business data into actionable insights through exploratory data analysis, KPI evaluation, and executive-level reporting. It demonstrates how data analytics can support strategic decision-making, revenue growth, customer acquisition, and business performance monitoring.

The project includes an Executive Decision Report in PDF format and a Jupyter Notebook documenting the analytical workflow.

## Objectives
- Analyze business and sales performance using data analytics.
- Identify key revenue drivers and potential business risks.
- Evaluate important business KPIs and trends.
- Develop actionable, data-driven strategic recommendations.
- Present findings in an executive-friendly report with ROI scenarios and an implementation timeline.

## Project Deliverables
1. **Executive Decision Report:** A PDF presentation/report summarizing findings, risks, recommendations, ROI scenarios, and the proposed implementation plan.
2. **Final Analytical Jupyter Notebook:** Documents data preparation, exploratory data analysis, KPI calculations, visualizations, and key insights.
3. **Dataset:** The business dataset used for the analysis, where applicable.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Revenue:** Total sales generated during the reporting period.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Revenue divided by the number of orders.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Acquisition spending divided by the number of new customers acquired.
- **Churn Rate:** Customers lost divided by customers at the start of the period, multiplied by 100.

## Methodology
1. Data loading and inspection.
2. Data cleaning and validation.
3. Exploratory data analysis and trend identification.
4. KPI calculation and business performance evaluation.
5. Visualization of important patterns and comparisons.
6. Identification of revenue opportunities and risk areas.
7. Development of strategic recommendations.
8. Preparation of the executive report and implementation roadmap.

## Strategic Recommendations
The report evaluates three areas for potential business improvement:

1. **Revenue Growth:** Identify high-performing products, categories, and regions to guide sales strategies.
2. **Customer Acquisition Efficiency:** Monitor acquisition spending and CAC to improve marketing efficiency.
3. **Customer Retention:** Track churn and customer trends to identify opportunities for retention initiatives.

Recommendations and projected benefits should be validated against actual business results before implementation.

## ROI & Implementation Plan
The report includes an illustrative ROI scenario and a phased implementation timeline. These projections are estimates based on stated assumptions, not guaranteed financial outcomes. Actual results may vary depending on business conditions, implementation costs, and customer behavior.
